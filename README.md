# Photos

A dump of photos from robotics competitions, a trip to Iceland, and some food shots at home.

## Picks

**iceland-blue-lagoon.png**
Milky blue water cutting through black lava rock. This one's the strongest of the Iceland batch, the color contrast does all the work.

**frc-match-03-crowd.png**
Match action with the crowd and referee in frame, balls mid-air. Good sense of scale and chaos.

**frc-match-06-blue-light.png**
Two robots paired up under blue stage lighting after a match. Moodier and more atmospheric than the straight action shots.

**food-sourdough-bread.png**
Loaf, flour bag, and rolling pin against a brick wall. Warm light, nice composition, could pass for a bakery ad.

**iceland-glacier.png**
Wide shot of a glacier tongue running between two mountains under heavy cloud cover. Captures how big and empty that part of the country feels.

## Everything else

- `frc-match-01.png` through `frc-match-09-rings.png`: match photos from FRC events, mostly team 5940
- `iceland-reykjavik.png`: overlook of the city and harbor with fog rolling over the hills
- `food-product-soy-sauce.png`, `food-basil-garlic-oil.webp`: ingredient shots
